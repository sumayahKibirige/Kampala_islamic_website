Our Journey

A History of Excellence

1990
Foundation

The School is Founded

A group of visionary Muslim scholars and community leaders established Kampala Islamic Secondary School in Bweyogerere, with just 3 classrooms and 87 pioneering students.

1998
Growth

First UCE Cohort Graduates

Our inaugural Senior 4 class sat the Uganda Certificate of Education examinations. 94% passed, with 12 students earning Division One — a remarkable debut that established our reputation.

2005
Expansion

Senior 5 & 6 (A-Level) Introduced

The school expanded to offer Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education, adding sciences, arts, and business streams — opening doors to Uganda's top universities for our graduates.

2015
Modernisation

New Science Labs & ICT Centre Open

A major infrastructure investment brought state-of-the-art science laboratories, a 60-seat computer lab, and a fully stocked library — cementing our commitment to 21st century learning.

2026
Today

1,200+ Students & Growing

KISS now serves over 1,200 students from across Uganda, with 80+ qualified teachers, a new mosque, and a Hifz programme — standing proud as one of Kampala's most respected schools.

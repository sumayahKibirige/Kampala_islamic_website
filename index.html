How to Apply

  1. 1

    Get the Form

    description

    Collect a copy from the school's admissions office in Bweyogerere.

  2. 2

    Fill & Attach

    edit_note

    Attach certified PLE or UCE results, a birth certificate and recent passport photos.

  3. 3

    Hand It In

    assignment_turned_in

    Return the completed form to the admissions office, where the application fee is paid.

  4. 4

    Interview & Enrol

    how_to_reg

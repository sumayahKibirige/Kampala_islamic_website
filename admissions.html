Simple Process

How to Apply

1
description

Get the Form

Collect a physical copy from the school's admissions office on Bweyogerere.

2
edit_note

Fill & Attach

Complete the form and attach certified copies of your PLE or UCE results slip, birth certificate, and 2 passport photos.

3
payments

Pay Application Fee

Pay a non-refundable application fee of UGX 20,000 via Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel) or at the school bursar's office.

4
how_to_reg

Interview & Enrol

Attend an entrance interview on the scheduled date. Successful applicants receive an admission letter within 5 working days.
